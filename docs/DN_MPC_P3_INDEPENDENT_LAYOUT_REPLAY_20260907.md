analytic DN-MPC + strict Joint CBF controller before JEPA or Ledger-Lite
integration.

## Failure diagnosis

The only failed episode was g3 episode 3:

- `initial_side_distance=9.0 m`;
- defender side `left`, defenders start at `x=-9.0`;
- target starts at `x=+9.0` and escapes in the `+x` direction;
- observation condition `delayed_noisy`, target motion `s_curve`;
- at step 94, the selected route and every other candidate were rejected by
  the strict CBF probe;
- active constraints included `boundary_upper_defender_2_axis_0`, pairwise
  constraints, and acceleration limits;
- the controller emitted `cbf_controlled_abort` with no physical violation.

This is a hard feasibility failure at the edge of the current bounded world,
not a raw-action collision. The result shows that the v7 route hysteresis is
promising but not yet robust to the extreme 9 m outward-escape slice. It does
not justify loosening CBF margins or disabling the abort gate.

## Decision gate

The P3 candidate passes the zero-defender-violation safety gate on 12 episodes,
but it does not pass a 100% safe-capture generalization gate. Do not integrate
JEPA or Ledger-Lite yet. The next engineering task is a feasibility-aware
planner audit for the 9 m outward-escape slice:

1. add an explicit boundary-rescue / radial-in candidate and verify its
   reachable projection;
2. record whether target belief age and target-boundary risk are visible to the
   public planner before the final abort;
3. replay the failed scene with the same manifest after the fix;
4. require zero defender safety violations and no increase in controlled abort
   before adding more seeds or a learned evaluator.

The current 91.7% is a development result, not a locked benchmark or a claim
that DN-MPC solves the full L0-L3 task.
