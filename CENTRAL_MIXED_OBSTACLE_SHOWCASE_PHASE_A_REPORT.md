# Central Mixed-Obstacle Showcase: Phase A Probe

## Purpose

This is a controlled, simulation-only distribution-shift probe for the S1
showcase scenario defined in `SHOWCASE_MIXED_OBSTACLE_CAPTURE_TODOLIST.txt`.
It is not a replacement for the existing formal locked test and does not alter
the F1/F2 conclusions.

The question is deliberately narrow: can the frozen F2 policy safely traverse
a fixed central region containing heterogeneous obstacles when the defenders
and target start on opposite sides? Before retraining, the same geometry is
also tested with the existing decentralized Dynamic Encirclement rule expert.

## Fixed Scenario

- World: `20 m x 20 m x 10 m`; defender and target boundary clamping remains
  active.
- Defenders: four agents at `x = -5.0 m`, distributed in `y = [-2.4, 2.4] m`.
- Target: one agent at `(5.0, 0.0, 4.2) m`, initially escaping toward positive
  `x`.
- Central obstacle zone: `x in [-2.5, 3.0] m`.
- Obstacles: one cylinder, one box, and one wall. Their exact shape, center,
  height, radius, and half extents are recorded in every `scenario.json` and
  `trajectory.npz` file.
- Capture predicate: a defender enters the unchanged `0.80 m` capture radius.
- Safety predicate: no defender obstacle/inter-agent collision and no boundary
  violation; the probe separately checks target-obstacle clearance.
- Showcase-only sensing: detection range is `14.0 m` so the deliberately
  distant opposite-side initial states remain partially observable. This is a
  stated distribution shift relative to the F2 formal configuration.

The map generator rejects out-of-bounds starts, overlapping starts, obstacle
overlap at spawn, and layouts without a conservative planar route from the
defender side to the target side.

## Protocol

- Base seed: `643001`; 20 consecutive episode seeds.
- Target speed scale: `0.55`.
- Frozen F2 checkpoint:
  `three_d_encirclement_stage4d_clean/results/stage4d_formal/`
  `f2_uncertainty_features/seed521001/recurrent_mappo/checkpoint.pt`.
- Both policy and expert use the same local CBF execution filter.
- The episode metrics and summary are generated by
  `scripts/evaluate_mixed_obstacle_showcase.py`.

## Results

| Method | Safe Capture | Defender zone crossing | Collision | Boundary violation | Mean capture time | Mean visible fraction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Frozen F2 + CBF | 0 / 20 (0%) | 100% | 0% | 0% | n/a | 0.770 |
| Dynamic Encirclement + CBF | 20 / 20 (100%) | 100% | 0% | 0% | 3.45 s | 0.703 |

Additional observed safety quantities:

- F2 + CBF: mean/worst defender minimum clearance `0.356 / 0.352 m`; target
  minimum obstacle clearance `3.726 m`.
- Dynamic Encirclement + CBF: mean/worst defender minimum clearance
  `0.477 / 0.463 m`; target minimum obstacle clearance `3.726 m`.
- The target is not required to cross the central zone in S1, so its reported
  zone-crossing rate is correctly `0%` for both methods.

## Interpretation

The controlled map has a safe local-information solution: the rule expert
achieves capture in all 20 episodes while every defender crosses the central
obstacle zone. The frozen F2 policy also crosses the zone safely in every
episode, but never closes to the capture radius within the 25-second horizon.

This is evidence of a stable distribution-shift failure caused by the
opposite-side start formation, long initial separation, and heterogeneous
central geometry. It is not evidence that the map is infeasible, and it does
not invalidate F2's existing formal locked-test result. It satisfies the
decision gate for the planned curriculum fine-tuning: retain F2 as the frozen
baseline, use the rule expert as the behavior-cloning teacher, and introduce
the central mixed layout gradually during new training runs.

## Reproduction

```powershell
python scripts/evaluate_mixed_obstacle_showcase.py `
  --method f2 `
  --checkpoint F:/uav_capture/three_d_encirclement_stage4d_clean/results/stage4d_formal/f2_uncertainty_features/seed521001/recurrent_mappo/checkpoint.pt `
  --output-dir results/showcase/probe_f2_cbf_d5_vis14_seed643001_n20 `
  --seed 643001 --episodes 20 `
  --initial-side-distance 5.0 --detection-range 14.0 `
  --target-speed-scale 0.55 --use-cbf --device cpu

python scripts/evaluate_mixed_obstacle_showcase.py `
  --baseline dynamic_encirclement `
  --output-dir results/showcase/probe_dynamic_cbf_d5_vis14_seed643001_n20 `
  --seed 643001 --episodes 20 `
  --initial-side-distance 5.0 --detection-range 14.0 `
  --target-speed-scale 0.55 --use-cbf --device cpu
```

Generated episode CSVs, checkpoints, and media remain under ignored
`results/` directories; the commands, fixed scenario implementation, and this
summary are versioned in Git.
